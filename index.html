/ 01 — About

I work at the intersection of statistics, Python, and business questions. Recent projects have included building a Nutrient Density Score across 175,000 USDA food products, deploying a live financial-data web tool, and modeling caloric variation across retail cheese SKUs.

My approach is straightforward: understand the question, clean the data carefully, choose the simplest method that answers it, and explain the result without jargon. I'm comfortable owning a problem from raw CSV to executive-ready chart — and equally comfortable saying when a model isn't trustworthy yet.

Outside of coursework, I've shipped a deployed product (stocksbybots.com), and I treat every project as practice for the work I want to do in industry: analytics that drive a real decision.
